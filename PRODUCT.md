# Product

## Register

product

## Users

High school students (Matric) in South Africa preparing for exams. They use the app on mobile devices, often in high-stress exam preparation periods. Need quick, accessible help with study materials and practice questions. Secondary audiences include teachers assigning work and parents tracking progress.

## Product Purpose

Lumni is an AI-powered South African Matric exam preparation platform — an offline-capable, mobile-first study companion for NSC candidates. It provides AI-generated questions, past paper practice, SM-2 flashcards, gamification, and comprehensive progress tracking. Success means students feel prepared, confident, and supported through their exam journey.

## Brand Personality

Playful & Energetic — approachable, encouraging, and confidence-building for stressed students. The interface should feel supportive and motivating, not cold or clinical. Voice: a knowledgeable friend who knows the material, not a proctor grading performance.

## Anti-references

- Cold clinical whites — pure gray reads like a hospital, not a study room
- Purple gradients, neon accents, or glassmorphism as decoration
- Generic SaaS landing page theatre (big numbers, smaller labels, gradient accents)
- Exam hall sterility — the student is studying, not being processed
- Anything that makes the student feel watched or judged

## Design Principles

1. **Playful but professional** — Energetic without being distracting
2. **Student-friendly** — Clear, easy to navigate, not overwhelming
3. **Encouraging** — UI should motivate, not intimidate
4. **Efficient** — Get students to answers quickly
5. **Accessible** — Works for all students, including those with disabilities

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance target
- Dark mode support for low-light study environments
- Reduced motion support
- Touch targets at least 44x44pt for mobile usability
- 11 South African language locales (en, af, zu, xh, st, tn, nso, ts, ss, ve, nd)
