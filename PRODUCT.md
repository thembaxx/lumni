## Design Context

### Users

High school students (Matric) in South Africa preparing for exams. They use the app on mobile devices, often in high-stress exam preparation periods. Need quick, accessible help with study materials and practice questions.

### Brand Personality

Playful & Energetic - approachable, encouraging, and confidence-building for stressed students. The interface should feel supportive and motivating, not cold or clinical.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Modern, friendly, student-focused design
- Both light and dark mode support
- Clean but with pops of color/energy
- Not overly serious - but still trustworthy

### Design Principles

1. **Playful but professional** - Energetic without being distracting
2. **Student-friendly** - Clear, easy to navigate, not overwhelming
3. **Encouraging** - UI should motivate, not intimidate
4. **Efficient** - Get students to answers quickly
5. **Accessible** - Works for all students, including those with disabilities
