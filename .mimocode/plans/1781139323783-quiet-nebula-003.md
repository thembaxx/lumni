## Why this matters

The story reader generates AI comprehension questions (`generateComprehensionQuestions` in `src/lib/stories/service.ts:66`) but renders them as inert `<p>` tags (`story-reader-client.tsx:185-188`). Students can't answer, grades aren't tracked, and comprehension scores don't feed into competency. The `Question` type already has `correctAnswer`, `explanation`, `bloomLevel`, and `marks` — the grading infrastructure is ready, just not wired.

### Step 3: Add competency tracking for comprehension

In `src/lib/competency-engine/`, add:

```ts

  userId: string,
  storyId: string,
  subjectId: string,
  scores: number[],

```

This maps average comprehension score to competency adjustment:

- avg >= 80: +0.1 proficiency
- avg 50-79: +0.05
- avg < 50: no change
