## Why this matters

Students encounter vocabulary words in lessons (`lesson-view-client.tsx:285-324`) and stories (`story-reader-client.tsx:144-148`) but can't save them for review. The existing flashcard engine (`src/lib/flashcard-engine/`) with SM-2 spaced repetition is the perfect backend — it just needs a vocabulary data source. This creates a "see word → save → review later" loop that's the core of language learning.
